# Privacy Policy for My Pets

**Last Updated: January 16, 2026**

Thank you for choosing **My Pets**. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information Collection
My Pets is designed to work primarily offline. Most of the data you enter (pet details, activities, health records, etc.) is stored locally on your device.

- **Personal Data**: We do not collect personal identifying information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number unless you explicitly provide it for feedback purposes via email.
- **Microchip/ID Data**: Any pet identification numbers or microchip IDs you enter are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
- **Media**: If you choose to upload a photo of your pet, it is stored locally or processed via local device storage.

## 2. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services which may collect data according to their own policies:

- **Google AdMob**: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to personalize ads.
- **Microsoft Clarity**: Used for usage analytics and improving user experience. It may track how you interact with the app.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
All your pet data is stored in a local database (SQLite) on your device. We do not have access to this data. If you delete the app or clear its data, this information will be permanently lost unless you have a backup.

## 4. Permissions
The app may request the following permissions:
- **Internet**: For ads and analytics.
- **Storage/Gallery**: To select and store pet photos.
- **Notifications**: To send health and activity reminders.
