# Privacy Policy - Hourglass Timer Feature

## Introduction
This privacy policy specifically covers the Hourglass Timer feature within the My Pets application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how this feature operates.

## Data Collection
The Hourglass Timer feature:
- **Does NOT collect any personal data**
- **Does NOT send any information to external servers**
- **Does NOT track your usage patterns**
- **Does NOT access your device's microphone, camera, or location**

## Local Storage
The Hourglass Timer feature may store the following information locally on your device:
- Timer duration preferences
- Last used timer settings
- Sound/vibration preferences

This information:
- Never leaves your device
- Is not shared with third parties
- Can be cleared by uninstalling the app

## Permissions
The Hourglass Timer feature may request the following permissions:
- **Vibration**: To provide haptic feedback when the timer completes
- **Audio**: To play system sounds for timer notifications (uses built-in system sounds only)

## Third-Party Services
The Hourglass Timer feature does not integrate with any third-party services or analytics platforms beyond those already disclosed in the main app privacy policy.

## Data Security
Since no data is transmitted or stored externally, your timer usage remains completely private and secure on your device.

## Changes to This Policy
Any changes to this privacy policy will be reflected in app updates and communicated through the app's update notes.

For questions about this privacy policy or the Hourglass Timer feature, please contact us at:
- Email: support@mypetsapp.com
- Website: https://mypetsapp.com/privacy
